# Speaker notes

These are concise delivery notes, not a transcript.

## Slide 15 — “Not who knows how to ask an AI to find it”

This section’s thesis was written before most of the data that follows: real debugging means holding a mental model of a complex system under pressure—forming, challenging, and falsifying hypotheses while the business is on fire.

Recent outages reinforced it for me. AI helped gather information and suggest paths, but it did not solve the problem. At times it reinforced an invalid assumption and made the investigation worse. Information is not understanding; the issue was resolved through a clear system model and judgment.

AI handles many easy first-pass cases well. The concern is that easy cases do not build debugging instinct. That comes from repeated production ownership and accountability.

Keep the later callback in reserve: *If AI tooling went dark during a major incident, how long would your team take to find the problem?*
